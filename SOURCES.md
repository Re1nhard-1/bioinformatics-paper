# Source data and software

- **Original cohort:** Kang et al., *Nature Biotechnology* (2018), [10.1038/nbt.4042](https://doi.org/10.1038/nbt.4042), [correction](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41587-020-0715-9); [GSE96583/GSM2560248](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE96583). The four retrieval URLs, sizes and SHA256 hashes are in `reproduction_integrated/environment/geo_sources.json`. The selected experiment uses six SLE donors cultured without interferon, not healthy controls. GEO source materials remain subject to submitter rights; this release does not apply its own CC BY license to those materials.
- **External cohort:** Perez et al., *Science* (2022), [10.1126/science.abf1970](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.abf1970), [correction](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.adr4064). The [fixed public CELLxGENE H5AD](https://datasets.cellxgene.cziscience.com/c55dc602-d168-4d15-acc1-5de4f2f5d551.h5ad) is object `c55dc602-d168-4d15-acc1-5de4f2f5d551`, 12,218,105,530 bytes; collection `436154da-bcf1-4130-9c8b-120ff9a888f2`, version `0340d8f6-a8ca-4ad8-8664-07338a8bf55f`. Its multipart ETag is in `reproduction_integrated/environment/external_source.json`; a full-object SHA256 is not asserted. Associated study accessions are GSE174188/GSE137029. CELLxGENE public submission terms use CC BY 4.0; retain original author/dataset attribution. No controlled genotypes are included.
- **Transformations:** select six broad PBMC types and eligible donors, retain the published coded cell/donor identifiers, extract raw counts, construct specified reference samples and synthetic targets, and calculate predictions and summaries. The external eligible inventory is 114,524 cells; original exported selections contain 93,892 cells, and the additional-draw union contains 106,359 cells. These are different, explicitly identified sets. The 840 external target mixtures are unchanged. Counts are synthetic sums, not measured bulk sequencing.
- **MuSiC:** Wang et al., *Nature Communications* (2019), [10.1038/s41467-018-08023-x](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-018-08023-x). Official MuSiC 1.0.0 at [commit f21fe67f5670d5e9fca0ad7550abaae3423eb59c](https://github.com/xuranw/MuSiC/tree/f21fe67f5670d5e9fca0ad7550abaae3423eb59c) is GPL (>=3), obtained separately. Installed third-party libraries and complete source H5AD files are not redistributed.

- **Measured bulk and flow cytometry:** Monaco et al., *Cell Reports* (2019), [10.1016/j.celrep.2019.01.041](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2019.01.041). RNA-seq: GSE107011/SRP125125; independent flow truth: Supplementary Table S6, worksheet GSE107019 (Our S13 cohort). recount3 supplies gene coverage counts; sources and transformations are in `data/processed/real_bulk/sources.json`. The post-hoc quantification pilot uses SRR6298350 (participant 453W), Ensembl GRCh37 release 87 and the retained 10x hg19 gene biotypes. Download URLs and preparation commands are in `analysis/prepare_salmon_pilot.py` and `analysis/run_salmon_pilot.sh`. Genome, full annotation and FASTQ downloads are obtained from their original providers.
