## Published-workflow comparison

Zhao et al. (doi:10.1093/bib/bbag184) report reprocessing GSE107011 FASTQ against a 10x hg19 reference with nf-core/RNA-seq 3.19.0 and rounded Salmon counts. Their public repository, anijudy/reference-mixture-matching-deconvolution at 8db33c4cebf21da891530c5f01e0f3d34815b7fa, reads salmon_counts_matrix.tsv, intersects both bulk and single-cell genes before calling unmodified music_prop, and does not specify a marker subset. It uses nine cell types and substantially more reference donors. Its comparison therefore does not isolate quantification. The repository excludes processed data; the inspected tree does not contain the FASTQ quantification commands or the exact 10x reference release. Source excerpts and provenance are in data/raw/real_bulk/zhao2026/.

The input-only gene-intersection check uses the same diagnostic reference and no new fits. Seven reference genes are absent from the current recount3 annotation; they account for 0.0243% of its pooled UMI counts. This small loss does not support prioritizing the intersection order as the explanation for the large failure. Results are in results/real_bulk_scale/gene_intersection_diagnostic.json; check_real_bulk_gene_intersection.py is its entry point. This is not a reconstruction of the unpublished Salmon gene set.

The completed bounded experiment quantified the already selected diagnostic sample 453W (SRR6298350; two ENA FASTQ files totaling 1,712,294,746 compressed bytes), using one fixed hg19 transcript annotation and documented Salmon settings, and compared with its saved baseline using the same reference. The paper does not provide enough reference-build detail to call this an exact reproduction. Ubuntu 24.04.5 and Salmon 1.10.3 are installed locally. The fixed pilot specification follows. Do not expand to all participants or budget conditions before the input and boundary-estimate diagnostics are assessed, and do not choose quantification settings by flow agreement.

Source: https://www.10xgenomics.com/support/software/cell-ranger/downloads/cr-ref-build-steps.

Use the full GRCh37 primary assembly as decoy sequence, concatenated after the reconstructed transcripts. Every genomic FASTA identifier is listed in decoys.txt. Reference construction uses no bulk expression or flow truth. Source: https://salmon.readthedocs.io/en/latest/salmon.html.

Before quantification, an attempted partial-decoy construction with MashMap 2.0 (80% identity, 500-base segments) failed on the whole genome with std::bad_alloc. A chromosome-group attempt fit within memory but had processed only about one fifth of transcripts in its first of nine groups after several minutes, implying several hours overall; it was stopped without a completed decoy set. The final workflow therefore uses Salmon's recommended full-genome decoys and a 22 GiB WSL RAM cap with 8 GiB swap. Those unfinished mappings are not scientific results or reused index inputs. This technical adaptation was made before generating any bulk quantification or MuSiC predictions; FASTQ, assembly, annotation, sample and quantification parameters remain fixed.

Process the paired FASTQs with fastp 0.23.4, paired-end adapter detection, minimum read length 25 and otherwise default filters. Quantify paired reads with Salmon --libType A --validateMappings --seqBias --gcBias, six threads and otherwise defaults. Command validation before quantification confirmed that Salmon 1.10.3 exposes no random-seed option; use its native inference behavior, with no bootstraps or Gibbs sampling, and retain the actual output rather than claiming bitwise repeatability. Preserve fastp and Salmon QC. Sum transcript NumReads by stable Ensembl gene ID, then round gene totals to the nearest integer (ties to even); do not substitute TPM or counts scaled by transcript length. Confirm transcript-to-gene coverage and count conservation. Retain zero-count annotated genes in the gene-count input.

## Pilot result

The completed 453W pilot retained 99.44% of reads and mapped 75.16% of the retained fragments. Both MuSiC fits converged after four iterations. B and T estimates remained zero under both input treatments; weighted MAE was 34.358 percentage points, unchanged from the saved baseline to numerical precision. Ordinary NNLS MAE was 38.087 points. Weighted NK estimates changed from 56.52% to 46.87%, with a corresponding increase in monocytes: unchanged MAE does not imply identical estimates. Shared-gene preprocessing retained 99.9757% of reference UMIs, and RPL/RPS-prefix genes contributed 1.546% of the new bulk counts on the original basis genes.

The prespecified recovery and accuracy gates were not met, so processing will not expand to the remaining participants. This single-sample comparison does not establish the cause of the transfer failure or reproduce the full published workflow. Preserve the saved quantification and fits; results and the diagnostic figure source are in results/real_bulk_salmon.
