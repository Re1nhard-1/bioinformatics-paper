# Reference-composition sensitivity follow-up v1

2026-09-17. This targeted follow-up is designed after all original external results and the integrated manuscript were seen. It is not a preregistered study, a new cohort or an independent biological replication. Freeze this file and the full reference plan before any follow-up predictions. Preserve the original ring, protocols, results and figures.

## Scope and controlled change

Test whether the signed weighted-MuSiC budget contrast remains similar under one new deterministic reference-donor grouping. Reuse exactly the original 14 normal/na donors, all 840 synthetic targets, 30,172 genes, three saved donor/type/reference-block cell prefixes and original input matrices. Only reference-donor grouping and the consequently omitted reference donor change. This does not test new reference-cell draws, population representativeness, real bulk data or additional methods.

Order the same 14 labels by ascending SHA256 of UTF-8 `music-external-reference-composition-v1|ring|{donor}`. Apply the original forward cyclic rule: for held-out ring position i, use positions i+1 through i+12, partition consecutively into four triples, and omit i+13 (all positions modulo 14). Each donor is held out once, omitted once and contributes to references in 12 folds. Use this one ordering, regardless of its overlap with the original or its results. Do not search alternative salts/rings. Record old/new triple overlap and all changed omissions.

Use budgets 60 and 300 cells/type and only balanced versus 10:1:1 allocation. Balanced quotas are 20/20/20 and 100/100/100; unequal quotas are 50/5/5 and 250/25/25. Rotate every member of each triple as dominant. Reuse original blocks 0,1,2 and donor/type prefixes, indexed in the original donor order; never reinterpret prefix axes using the new ring. Targets and expression are unchanged. Cell subsets are nested across budgets within an allocation, not necessarily across allocations. Retain full count/library information and original cell identities.

The plan contains 14 unique reference triples, 24 reference matrices per triple, 336 references, 1,344 held-out/reference cases, 80,640 weighted fits and 161,280 scientific predictions including ordinary NNLS. Each unique reference applies to four held-out donors (240 fixed targets). This restricted check omits the middle budget and middle allocations by design; it is not a replacement for the original full grid.

## Endpoints and reporting

The primary descriptive endpoint is unchanged: I_weighted(300)-I_weighted(60), where I(B)=MAE(10:1:1)-MAE(balanced), in percentage points. Average equally over 60 targets, three blocks, four triples, three dominant choices for unequal allocations, then over 14 donors. Share each balanced baseline across its three paired contrasts, without treating them as independent data. Report every donor's original and follow-up endpoint, paired change between designs, full range and sign counts. No population P values or confidence intervals: the same donors, targets and reference prefixes remain shared.

Report both methods' absolute MAE, RMSE and type-specific absolute errors at all four budget/allocation combinations; signed I(B), weighted-minus-NNLS I(B), all donor/block and donor/triple/dominant I(B), all reference-block-specific budget contrasts, and counts of positive/negative effects. Interpret a negative contrast as a downward shift of the signed allocation effect, not necessarily a smaller absolute sensitivity or improved absolute accuracy. No equivalence margin or minimum effect size is invented after seeing the result.

Assess agreement descriptively by the aggregate endpoint's sign and size, the full donor-paired distribution, and reference/block heterogeneity. Agreement supports stability across these two fixed grouping designs only. If the mean reverses, attenuates substantially or individual effects change, report the discrepancy and narrow the manuscript claim; do not run successive rings until it agrees. A small negative I(300) must not be presented as proof of harmless imbalance. All outputs are retained regardless of outcome. After this one bounded check, return to manuscript interpretation and release preparation; another experiment requires a separately documented substantive reason.
